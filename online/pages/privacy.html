Hosting (Cloudflare)

Kurviq runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). To deliver the website, Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical request data. Cloudflare is our processor (Art. 28 GDPR) and also processes some network and security data for its own purposes. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) and Cloudflare's standard contractual clauses. We keep no request logs. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (a secure, working website).

In your browser

Scenes, settings and your sign-in state are stored in your browser (localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, the service worker's cache). This is strictly necessary for the app (§25(2) No. 2 TDDDG); you can delete it by clearing this site's data. Local scenes stay there unless you put them in Google Drive or connect an agent. Embedded content in a scene (for example YouTube or Figma), linked images, the shared libraries and fallback fonts are loaded by your browser directly from their providers, whose privacy policies apply.

Google sign-in and Google Drive (optional)

When you sign in with Google (Google Ireland Limited; Google is responsible for your account itself), Kurviq gets your name, email address, profile picture and account id, and access only to Drive files it creates or that you open with it (drive.file; drive.install adds "Open with Kurviq" in Drive). Two cookies are set, both strictly necessary (§25(2) No. 2 TDDDG): kq_google, encrypted, holds Google's sign-in so you stay signed in (400 days, renewed on use; the local install keeps it in its browser storage for 90 days instead), and kq_oauth_state protects the sign-in (1 hour). Our server only issues one-hour access tokens and stores none, except for live sync (below). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. "Sign out" ends it in this browser; myaccount.google.com/permissions removes Kurviq's access altogether.

Live sync of scenes in Drive

When you edit a scene stored in Google Drive, every change goes to our server (a Cloudflare Durable Object for that scene), which passes it to the others who have the scene open and saves the scene to the Drive file about once a minute, with your Google access. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

  • Drawing content, images, fonts, settings and the scene name are encrypted in your browser with a key that is stored only on the Drive file; our database cannot read them. The server stores in readable form only what it needs to keep everyone's copy consistent: element ids, types, versions, connections, groups and frames, the ids of images and fonts, your Google account id and pseudonym, and times. While it saves, it decrypts the scene in memory. Your display name is kept only while you are connected and is shown to the others who have the scene open, as is theirs to you.
  • So that your changes reach Drive after you close the tab, the server keeps your Google refresh token, encrypted with a key outside the database: until your changes are in Drive, at most 4 hours after your last connection. While you are connected, it checks your access every 10 minutes and Google notifies it of changes to the file. When you sign out and Google does not confirm right away that Kurviq's access is revoked, the server keeps the encrypted token only to retry the revocation, until Google confirms it, at most 30 days.
  • Retention: unsaved changes until they are in Drive (usually within a minute; changes that cannot be saved under their author's name at most 90 days), the encrypted copy of the scene until 10 minutes after the last person left, technical records (tab ids, counters, save records) 30 days, records of "sign out everywhere" 400 days. Cloudflare can restore deleted data for up to 30 days.
  • Each element in the Drive file records its last author as a pseudonym that differs from scene to scene.

Shared and public scenes (optional)

Sharing uses Google Drive's permissions; Google stores who has access and emails the link (kurviq.com/s/<file id>/<name>). People you share with load the scene from Google with their own sign-in and, for live-synced scenes, connect to our server as above. To open a file shared with you, Kurviq may show Google's Picker (a script from Google LLC, USA). For scenes shared with "Anyone with the link", our server reads the file for viewers and Cloudflare caches it for one minute.

Fonts from Google Fonts (optional)

When you add a Google font, our server fetches it from Google (cached by Cloudflare for a day), so Google doesn't see you.

AI agents (optional)

Only if you connect an agent (MCP button) and allow it in Kurviq: the agent signs in with Kurviq (one-hour access tokens, renewable for up to 90 days) and its requests and your Kurviq tab's answers (for example drawing content and images) pass through our server, without being stored. What the agent's provider does with the data is governed by its terms. "Sign out all agents" ends every agent's access.

Feedback and contact form (optional)

Feedback reports (bug reports, change requests, also sent by your agent) are stored with the scene name you had open and the time, and deleted after 2 years. Contact form messages are stored with your name (optional), email address and message, emailed to us, and deleted after 1 year. For both, a keyed hash of your IP address is kept for about a day to limit how many messages one sender can send. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (improving Kurviq) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (answering you). Please don't put personal data into reports.

Usage statistics

From the page requests your browser makes anyway, our server counts totals per hour: page views by page type, country (from Cloudflare) and device class, and how often features are used (sign-ins, live connections, public scene views, agent tool calls by name, feedback and contact messages). Nothing is stored in or read from your browser for this. To estimate unique visitors per day, your IP address and browser string are turned into a one-way hash with a key that exists for one day only and added to a counter that cannot be reversed; at the end of the day only the estimated number remains. We store no IP addresses, identifiers or scene data for statistics, only the totals. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (understanding how Kurviq is used).

Right to object

Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (hosting, feedback, statistics), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR); write to us.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, e.g. the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und für das Recht auf Akteneinsicht Brandenburg. You don't have to provide any data; without your IP address the website cannot be delivered, without Google sign-in the Drive features are unavailable. There is no automated decision-making.

Live-Synchronisierung von Szenen in Drive

Wenn du eine Szene in Google Drive bearbeitest, geht jede Änderung an unseren Server (ein Cloudflare Durable Object für diese Szene); er gibt sie an die anderen weiter, die die Szene geöffnet haben, und speichert die Szene etwa einmal pro Minute mit deinem Google-Zugriff in die Drive-Datei. Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO.

  • Inhalt, Bilder, Schriften, Einstellungen und der Name der Szene werden in deinem Browser mit einem Schlüssel verschlüsselt, der nur an der Drive-Datei liegt; unsere Datenbank kann sie nicht lesen. Lesbar speichert der Server nur, was er braucht, damit alle Kopien übereinstimmen: Element-IDs, -Arten, Versionen, Verbindungen, Gruppen und Rahmen, die IDs von Bildern und Schriften, deine Google-Konto-ID und dein Pseudonym sowie Zeitpunkte. Beim Speichern entschlüsselt er die Szene im Arbeitsspeicher. Deinen Anzeigenamen behält er nur, solange du verbunden bist, und zeigt ihn den anderen, die die Szene geöffnet haben, so wie ihre dir.
  • Damit deine Änderungen auch nach dem Schließen des Tabs in Drive ankommen, bewahrt der Server dein Google-Refresh-Token auf, verschlüsselt mit einem Schlüssel außerhalb der Datenbank: bis deine Änderungen in Drive sind, höchstens 4 Stunden nach deiner letzten Verbindung. Solange du verbunden bist, prüft er alle 10 Minuten deinen Zugriff, und Google meldet ihm Änderungen an der Datei. Wenn du dich abmeldest und Google den Widerruf von Kurviqs Zugriff nicht sofort bestätigt, behält der Server das verschlüsselte Token nur, um den Widerruf zu wiederholen, bis Google ihn bestätigt, höchstens 30 Tage.
  • Speicherdauer: ungespeicherte Änderungen, bis sie in Drive sind (meist innerhalb einer Minute; Änderungen, die nicht unter dem Namen ihres Urhebers gespeichert werden können, höchstens 90 Tage), die verschlüsselte Kopie der Szene bis 10 Minuten nachdem die letzte Person gegangen ist, technische Daten (Tab-IDs, Zähler, Speicherprotokolle) 30 Tage, Vermerke zu „Sign out everywhere" 400 Tage. Cloudflare kann gelöschte Daten bis zu 30 Tage lang wiederherstellen.
  • Jedes Element in der Drive-Datei vermerkt seinen letzten Bearbeiter als Pseudonym, das in jeder Szene anders ist.

Nutzungsstatistik

Dafür wird nichts in deinem Browser gespeichert oder ausgelesen. Um eindeutige Besucher pro Tag zu schätzen, werden deine IP-Adresse und Browserkennung mit einem Schlüssel, der nur einen Tag existiert, zu einem Einweg-Hash verrechnet und in einen nicht umkehrbaren Zähler eingetragen; am Tagesende bleibt nur die geschätzte Zahl. Für die Statistik speichern wir keine IP-Adressen, Kennungen oder Szenendaten, nur die Summen. Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO (verstehen, wie Kurviq genutzt wird).

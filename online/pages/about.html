Kurviq: a free online whiteboard with slides, Google Drive sync and AI agents

Kurviq is a free whiteboard in your browser, built on the excellent Excalidraw. Sketch diagrams with a hand-drawn look, keep many drawings as named scenes, present them as slides, write formatted text, and let AI agents draw with you. No account needed: your drawings stay in your browser, or in your own Google Drive.

Everything Excalidraw does, plus:

  • Scenes: many drawings, each with a name and its own address, like kurviq.com/roadmap. Switch, rename, duplicate or delete them in the Scenes sidebar.
  • Google Drive sync: sign in with Google and every scene is a file in a folder "Kurviq" in your Drive, saved by itself a few seconds after you stop drawing, on all your devices. Kurviq sees only the files it made or you opened with it.
  • Work together: share a scene with people by email, as in Google Drive ("Can edit" or "Can view"). Their changes are merged with yours element by element, live.
  • Share a link: a link for the people you added, or for everyone (no sign-in needed to view or present), with short links like kurviq.com/pub/my-talk.
  • AI agents that draw with you: connect Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Claude or any MCP client. Your agent reads, draws and edits your scenes while you watch; your own changes are never overwritten.
  • Slides: every frame is a slide. Present fullscreen with a laser pointer, a presenter view with notes and timer, click-by-click steps, a template for header, footer and logo, and a slide sorter.
  • Formatted text: bold, italic, underline, colors, highlights, bullets, numbered lists, checklists and links in one text, with the usual shortcuts; pasting from Google Docs keeps the formatting.
  • Any font: any Google Fonts family or your own font file; it travels with the scene.
  • PDF export: one page per slide, real selectable text, small files.
  • Private: no account, no ads, no tracking. It works offline and can be installed as an app.
  • Open source: MIT licensed, on GitHub. Run it on your own machine with the same Drive sync and a local MCP server for agents.

Who it is for

People who think in boxes and arrows: software architects drawing systems, consultants sketching processes, speakers who want slides that look hand-made, teams planning on one shared canvas, and anyone who wants an AI agent to turn an idea into a diagram they can keep editing by hand.

How AI agents work with Kurviq

Kurviq is an MCP server. Click MCP in the app and give the link to your agent. The agent works through your open Kurviq tab: it reads what you drew, adds and changes elements, checks a deck for cut-off or low-contrast text, and gets a picture of every change. You keep drawing at the same time; Kurviq merges both. Details for agents: llms.txt.

Questions

Is Kurviq free?
Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads.
Where are my drawings stored?
In your browser. If you sign in with Google, also in your own Google Drive, one .excalidraw file per scene. On their way to Drive, live changes pass through Kurviq's server encrypted; it cannot read them. See privacy.
Do I need an account?
No. Sign in with Google only if you want sync across devices, sharing or live collaboration.
Can I open my Excalidraw files?
Yes. Kurviq reads and writes the normal .excalidraw format, so your drawings also open in Excalidraw.
Which AI agents work with Kurviq?
Any client that speaks MCP (Model Context Protocol), for example Claude Code, Claude, Codex and Cursor.
Is Kurviq made by the Excalidraw team?
No. Kurviq is an unofficial extension of Excalidraw by Fabian Wesner, not affiliated with Excalidraw.
